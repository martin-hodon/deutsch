# SITUATIONS (sentences/STORIES.md): one concrete scene of a setting -- where it is, who else is there, the things in
# it, what someone wants there and what can go wrong with the things that needs. The stories (trainer/story.js) take
# the words that carry the plot only from here; the settings stay the pool for the rest. A block:
#
#   SITUATION name : setting -- what happens
#     place   Küche | Esszimmer                 where (one of them)
#     people  kin | Freund                      the second person: kin (ihr Bruder, seine Mutter ...) or nouns
#     things  Tisch | Teller | Tasse             what is there
#     want    hungrig : Brot toasten ; uses Toaster    a STATE, the action it makes one want (object + verb), the
#                                                       things that action needs; the object as it is first said:
#                                                       bare (Brot toasten), ein (ein Buch lesen -- then: das
#                                                       Buch), der (den Tisch decken: the one here), sein (sein
#                                                       Gesicht waschen: her own), a plural (Kekse backen), or a
#                                                       verb with sich (sich ausruhen)
#     own     Fahrrad | Regenschirm             what she has WITH HER (said ihr Fahrrad); things of the place stay in `things`
#     trouble Toaster kaputt : reparieren       a thing, a bad OBJSTATE it can get, the action that mends it
#     trouble Auto kaputt : reparieren by Mechaniker     (by: who mends it; else the second person)
#     lack    Mehl : Supermarkt [holen]     what she may not have (a thing the action uses, or its object) and
#                                           where she gets it (kaufen, if no verb is said)

SITUATION frühstück : kitchen -- morgens frühstücken
  place   kuchyňa
  people  kin
  things  stôl | tanier | šálka | sklenica | hriankovač | kávovar | chlieb | káva | mlieko
  want    hladný : verb=hriankovať patient=none:chlieb ; uses hriankovač
  want    smädný : verb=variť patient=none:káva ; uses kávovar | šálka
  want    smädný : verb=piť patient=none:mlieko ; uses sklenica
  trouble hriankovač pokazený : opraviť
  trouble kávovar pokazený : opraviť
  trouble šálka špinavý : oplachovať
  trouble sklenica špinavý : oplachovať
  lack    chlieb : pekáreň
  lack    mlieko : supermarket
  lack    káva : supermarket
