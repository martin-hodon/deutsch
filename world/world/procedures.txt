# PROCEDURES (tools/world.py): what doing a wish IS, step by step -- told instead of one plain sentence ("Später briet
# sie die Frikadellen."), so a story shows the work: Sie schaltete den Herd ein und stellte die Pfanne darauf. Vorsichtig
# legte sie die Frikadellen in die heiße Pfanne. Die Frikadellen zischten. The trouble of the story hits the step that
# uses its thing (Als sie den Herd einschalten wollte, ...), the steps after it wait until it is mended.
#
#   PROCEDURE verb : what of | what of ; here Noun | Noun/Noun
#       verb: the wished action; `of`: categories of its object (categories.txt) or nouns; `here`: what must be there
#       (the situation's things or uses, the place's things) -- Noun/Noun: either
#     step    clause ; gives Noun:state        she does it (no subj=: the story's person)
#     process clause ; gives ...               a thing does it (subj= the thing): Die Butter schmolz.
#     key     clause                           the decisive step (at most one) -- of a cook, a craftsman: not a sip of
#                                              water (Jetzt kam der entscheidende Augenblick.)
#     close   clause                           her last touch, told as the end (Sie biss in den warmen Kuchen.)
#     step?   (process?, key?)                 a detail: told only in complex stories
#     ; senses   telling it, she has tasted / seen it (a verdict is earned)   ; moment   a key worth "Jetzt kam der
#     entscheidende Augenblick"   ; fetch   left out when its thing is there already (holte ihr Fahrrad)
#   The clauses are in the format of sentences/tests.txt without subject and tense: verb=, A=, D=, pp=prep:Noun,
#   manner= ... A noun is a thing of the story (der Herd once it is there, ein Glas until then); `obj` is the wished
#   thing; Noun/Noun the one the situation has; ~Noun: only where it is (else the line is left out); a value with its
#   own article (indef:Schluck) is said as it stands; new:obj a new one (einen neuen Stift), own:Noun hers, bare:obj
#   the wish's thing with no article and what is done to it (nach gebratenen Frikadellen). `gives`: an OBJSTATE the thing has after it (Pfanne:heiß) --
#   then said with it (in die heiße Pfanne); the verbs' own conditions (conditions.txt gives A:...) count too.
