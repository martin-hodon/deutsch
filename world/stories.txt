# THE STORIES (sentences/STORIES.md): a little world -- a SITUATION (sentences/situations/*.txt), a PLACE with its
# weather and properties, a person of the CAST with her likes (sentences/world/*) -- and what happens in it, told by
# trainer/story.js from the blocks below. Nothing German is in the code: every sentence is a block here.
#
# PLOT name {weight} : TOKENS -- the order of a story;   PART name : TOKENS {w} | TOKENS {w} -- alternatives
#     X     must be told (no way of telling it: no story)
#     X?    told half the time
#     (X)   told when the world has it (a way whose `when` holds)
#     X*    told while it can (WORK*: the procedure's lines, world/procedures.txt -- up to the thing in the way, and
#           after it is mended the rest)
# A PART's alternative is chosen among those whose first must-token can be told now.
#
# MOVE name {weight} when COND COND sets FLAG : the words around the clauses (S: of sentences/tests.txt)
#   then its clauses, one per line, in the tests.txt format, with
#     $slot      a value of the world (story.js SLOTS): $p1 $p2 (the people, er/sie when clear), $trouble ...
#     $slot?     dropped when the world has none
#     $frag      alone: several parts at once ($state: verb=sein pred=adj:müde; $act: the wanted action)
#     @list      a word of a WORDS list (not the one used last)
# A move has one block per way of telling it; the ways whose `when` holds are chosen by weight (story.js CONDS);
# `level N`: only in stories of complexity N or more (1 simple, 2 medium, 3 complex). `sets` marks the world (WISH is
# not told again after a STATE that said it).
#
# JOIN A B {chance} when COND : words -- B told right after A becomes ONE sentence with it, as an author would combine them
# (`when`: a cond of the world, or of the two clauses: same-subj -- one doer, B's subject may drop --, durative-a /
# durative-b -- that clause's verb takes time, story.durative: während needs it)
# (the chance grows with the complexity: none in simple stories). Its clauses: `ID = A` / `ID = B` -- that move's
# clause, changed (a TYPE: SUB conj=nachdem, INVERTED; key=value replaces; -key leaves it out), or a new clause with
# $slots; none given: 1 = A, 2 = B. Only plain ways join (one clause, `1 .`).

# THE PLAN (story.js): a PLOT is what happens; its lines are its ARCS -- the shapes it is told in (PLOT order/rush:
# the plot `order` in the shape `rush`), chosen by weight. A BEAT is a part of the story chosen when it is PLANNED,
# before a word is told -- so the plan knows what it holds (an obstacle in an order: the work waits for it); its
# [conds] are of the world at the start, `-` an empty way; ~tag: weighed by the narrator's taste for it. (A PART is
# chosen while the story is told.) The session remembers the arcs and beats told lately: less likely again.
#
# NARRATOR name {w} : how a story is told -- joins (x how often two moves become one sentence), maybe (an optional
# move told), decor (a thing said with its state: die heiße Pfanne), adorn (its colour, size), manner (how she does
# it), speech (x ways with speech); other keys weigh the ways tagged ~key: scene (the place's sounds and sights),
# talk (dialogue first and last), quiet (a quiet end), inner (her state shown first)
NARRATOR plain {2} : joins=.6 maybe=.4 decor=.35 adorn=.2 manner=.35 speech=.8 scene=.5 quiet=.7
NARRATOR lively {2} : joins=.9 maybe=.55 speech=1.8 talk=3 scene=.7 quiet=.5
NARRATOR painter {2} : joins=1.3 maybe=.65 decor=.75 adorn=.5 manner=.65 speech=.7 scene=3 quiet=2
NARRATOR warm {1.5} : joins=1.1 maybe=.6 speech=1.2 talk=1.5 inner=3 quiet=1.5

# ---- the beats
# (the scene first, then she is placed in it: In der Küche duftete es nach frischem Brot. Lisa stand am Herd.)
BEAT OPEN : [scene there-at complex ~scene] SCENE PLACED (OWN) {4} | WOPEN {5} | SET AMBIENCE* (JOB) (OWN) (THERE) (DESCRIBE) {2} | SET AMBIENCE* (OWN) (THERE) {3} | GOTO AMBIENCE* (OWN) (THERE) {3}

BEAT WANTFIRST : [~inner] (PAST) STATE SET (OWN) (THERE) {3} | [goto] GOTO (OWN) (THERE) {2}
# (the end of a wish: she does it, says what it is like, eats it, how she is; or the one who helped is thanked last)
BEAT WANTEND : (DONE) (VERDICT) COMMENT? (FINISH) REACT? (RESULT) {4} | [~quiet] (DONE) (FINISH) (RESULT) {2} | [p2 ~talk] (DONE) (VERDICT) (FINISH) (THANKEND) {1}
# (where she is when the order comes: the scene and its sounds; the time of day; or simply where she is)
BEAT SETTING : [scene workthing complex ~scene] SCENE AMBIENCE* ATWORK {4} | [workthing complex] ATTIME {2} | [workthing complex] ATWORK (AMBIENCE) {2} | SET (AMBIENCE) {1}
# (after the asking: where she stands, the scene)
BEAT SETTING-AFTER : [workthing complex] ATWORK (AMBIENCE) {3} | [scene complex ~scene] SCENE AMBIENCE* {2} | SET {1}
# (the asking: a plain question; a birthday first; hunger)
BEAT ASKING : REQUEST (REPLY) {3} | [baked complex] OCCASION REQUEST (REPLY) {1.5}
# (in a hurry: guests are coming, the food must be ready -- impatience shown)
BEAT RUSHING : RUSH (VOICE) (REPLY) {3} | RUSH (REPLY) (VOICE) {1}
# (it goes wrong in the work: a thing is in the way, or something is missing -- fetched)
BEAT KITCHEN-TROUBLE : [trouble] BLOCK FEEL? MEND {2} | [lack] LACK FETCH BACK? {2}
BEAT KITCHEN-TROUBLE-ALONE : [lack] LACK FETCH BACK? {1}
# (she tastes: the dish lacks a spice; or her verdict; or only what it is like now)
BEAT TASTING : (DONE) (LACKS) (ADDSPICE) (RETASTE) (HINT) (VERDICT) {3} | (DONE) (HINT) (VERDICT) {2} | (DONE) {1}
# (the end: served, tasted, the quiet hands; praised aloud; later, together at the table)
BEAT ENDING : SERVE (REACT) {3} | [~quiet] SERVE (REACT) (APRON) {2} | [~talk] SERVE PRAISE {2} | [~quiet] SERVE (REACT) LATER {1.5}
# (in a hurry: the guests come in time -- relief; the effort was worth it; or their praise)
BEAT RUSH-ENDING : (ARRIVAL) SERVE (REACT) (RELIEF) {2} | (ARRIVAL) SERVE (REACT) (RELIEF) (REFLECT) {1.5} | [~talk] (ARRIVAL) SERVE PRAISE (RELIEF) {2} | [~quiet] (ARRIVAL) SERVE (REACT) (APRON) {1}
BEAT FAVOR-END : THANKED (RESULT) {3} | [~talk] THANKED PRAISEFIX {1}

# ---- the parts: chosen while the story is told
PART WOPEN : (SEASON) WEATHER (TASTE) SET (WEAR) (OWN) (THERE)
PART WANT : (PAST) (FOND) STATE (WISH) {3} | (PAST) (FOND) STATE+WISH {2}
# (the mending told step by step when there is a procedure for it -- one asked or called does it, or she herself; a
#  relative never helps unasked; else in one sentence, as before)
PART MEND : [mend p2.here] ASK OFFER? FIXWORK* THANK? {4} | [mend p2.away] CALL ARRIVE FIXWORK* THANK? {3} | [mend self] FIXWORK* {3} | [!mend] ASK? HELP THANK? {2} | [!mend] CALL ARRIVE THANK? {1} | [!mend] CALLREL FIX THANK? {1} | [!mend] SELF {3}
# the procedure's lines (world/procedures.txt): a step of hers, a thing that does something, the decisive step
PART WORK : STEP {3} | PROCESS {2} | MOMENT? KEY {2} | HEAT {1} | MEANWHILE {1} | CHECKIN ANSWER {1} | SPICE {1} | SPATTER {1} | SMOKE {1} | DONENESS {20}
# the mending's lines -- done by the one who helps (or her) while she waits
PART FIXWORK mend : STEP {3} | PROCESS {2} | MOMENT? KEY {2} | WAIT {1}
