# THE SETTINGS (sentences/README.md): where words belong -- a sentence in SCENE MODE picks one setting and takes its
# nouns and verbs from it (and from the settings above and below it, and from scenes/general.txt), so that all its
# clauses are about one situation. A line:
#     SETTING name : parent -- what it is (roles: role role)
# A word belongs to settings by the files in scenes/ (scenes/restaurant.txt: `N Kellner : staff`); a ROLE is what a
# person does there (the guest orders, the staff brings) -- a verb with a role takes only subjects of that role.
# Adjectives and adverbs (colours, sizes, gern) belong to every setting; their categories decide where they fit.

# THE STATES that conditions (conditions.txt) speak of, and how a clause says one -- an adjective of the subject (sein
# + adj: weil er müde ist), a noun it has (haben + the bare noun: weil er Hunger hat); its OPPOSITE (obwohl er satt
# ist, isst er); OF: the objects it is about, when the action has one (hungrig: food -- "weil er Hunger hat, bestellt er
# eine Suppe", not "... Wasser"):
# GOAL: a state one acts FOR (um ... zu: Er arbeitet, um einzukaufen -- Geld; not "Er rennt, um einzuschlafen");
# FEELING: a mood -- a reason (weil er froh ist, lacht er), never a step in time (not "Nachdem er erschrocken war,
# schrie er vor Freude"). An OPPOSITE only where it makes the action surprising, not impossible (müde has none:
# "schläft, obwohl er wach ist" contradicts itself):
# DEFAULT: the normal state -- never a reason (not "rennt, weil er gesund ist", "zahlt, weil er Geld hat"), only what
# is against it (obwohl er krank ist) or a condition in the Konjunktiv II (Wenn ich Zeit hätte, ...); PERSON: only a person's (not "Die Zebras hatten Zeit");
# WISH: said only with what one wants (Er hatte Lust, einen Film zu sehen -- not "Er hatte Lust."); ENDS: the action
# that ends it (hungrig: essen -- a story whose action only prepares the food tells it too: Dann aß sie die Torte.):
#     STATE name : adj word | noun word ; opposite name ; of category category ; goal ; feeling ; default ; person ; wish ; ends verb
