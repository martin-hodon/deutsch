# the word-order exercise offers, one or two SIMPLE plans in the language-free clause format (world/CLAUSES.md) that
# show that structure and nothing beyond the base (present, objects, predicates), a time/place/manner word, and what
# the structure implies (structures.js IMPLIED). Every language's realizer says them: the sentence a structure always
# has in the Syntax exercise when the planner finds none, the examples of the grammar page's overview, and -- a plan
# a realizer cannot say -- which structures a language does not have. A block: the structure's name on its own line,
# its plan under it (S: the clause ids and marks; one clause line each); a `#` line is a note. The de.* choices are
# German's, the other languages ignore them. `-ru -zh` after the name: languages whose realizer does not say this plan
# (no such construction, or said wrong) -- they are not offered the structure through it.
