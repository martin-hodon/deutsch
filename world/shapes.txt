# THE SHAPES OF A SENTENCE (DESIGN.md): which clauses a sentence has and how they are joined -- language-free; the
# planner (engine/sentences.js) expands S, plans each clause, and the language's realizer says them in its order
# (German: de/rules/grammar.txt -- MAIN -> FRONT vfin MIDDLE ...). The clause kinds are the planner's (INVERTED: a
# statement after a clause in front; DROP: its subject left out; JE / DESTO: the more ..., the more ...; CONDV: an
# unreal condition first; WERREL: who ..., he ...). Weights {simple/medium/complex}; @feature: the target language's
# syllabus must have it (its grammar.txt FEATURE lines). coord, dropcoord, zwarmark, w.<word>: the joining words.
CLAUSES: MAIN INVERTED SUB REL YESNO WH IMP ZU REP DROP ZUADV JE DESTO CONDV WERREL

# ---- sentences -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# (the subordinate clause's link, a Konjunktiv II condition -- Wenn ich Zeit hätte, würde ich kommen -- and the like
#  are the planner's; the shapes are here)
# (complexity 1: one clause; 2: now and then two; 3: often two or three -- more clauses, more chances of nonsense: a
#  blind critic found sentences of 3 or 4 clauses all nonsense, of one clause with nothing added most often fine)
S -> MAIN "."                                {6/6/5}

S -> MAIN dropcoord DROP "."                 {0/0.4/0.8}  @subject-drop
S -> MAIN "," ZUADV "."                      {0/0.3/0.6}  @purpose
S -> w.entweder INVERTED "," w.oder MAIN "." {0/0.15/0.3} @paired
S -> zwarmark MAIN "," w.aber MAIN "."       {0/0.15/0.3} @paired
S -> JE "," DESTO "."                        {0/0.15/0.3} @so-that-the-more
S -> CONDV "," INVERTED "."                  {0/0.1/0.2}  @verb-first-if
S -> WERREL "," MAIN "."                     {0/0.1/0.2}  @free-relative
