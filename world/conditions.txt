# THE CONDITIONS (sentences/README.md): what an action NEEDS and what it GIVES -- states (settings.txt: hungrig, müde,
# krank, geld ...), the object's state (A:kaputt), or something had (has:Rechnung; has:A -- the action's object). Clause
# links follow from them: weil -- what the action needs (Er isst, weil er Hunger hat); obwohl -- its opposite (Obwohl
# er satt ist, isst er); deshalb -- what it gives (Er hat gearbeitet, und deshalb ist er müde); nachdem / bevor -- one
# action's gift is the other's need; um ... zu -- what one gives is what the other needs (Er spart, um ... zu kaufen).
