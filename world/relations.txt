# RELATIONS (DESIGN.md): what a free phrase of place, time or manner says, language-free -- the stories' and
# procedures' pp=RELATION:NOUN (pp=onto:sporák), the planner's place and time phrases. Each language says them with
# its prepositions (`<lang>/rules/relations.txt`: de onto -> auf A, an A by the noun's kind of place; en onto, on).
#
#   RELATION name -- what it is (German / English)
#
# where (a place is IN something -- a room, a box --, ON a surface or an open area, AT an edge or side: the noun's
# kind of place, world/meaning flags, decides; in/on/at is the relation as such)
RELATION in        -- inside, where (in der Küche / in the kitchen)
RELATION into      -- inside, where to (in die Küche / into the kitchen)
RELATION on        -- on a surface or an open area, where (auf dem Tisch, auf dem Markt / on the table, at the market)
RELATION onto      -- onto it, where to (auf den Tisch / onto the table)
RELATION at        -- at a side, an edge, a place one is by (am Fenster, an der Wand, am Strand / at the window, on the wall)
RELATION to-side   -- to a side, an edge, where to (an die Wand, ans Fenster / onto the wall, to the window)
RELATION to        -- to a place, a person, an event (zum Bahnhof, zu ihr, nach Wien / to the station, to her, to Vienna)
RELATION from      -- out of a place or from a person (aus dem Haus, von ihr / out of the house, from her)
RELATION off       -- off a surface (vom Tisch / off the table)
RELATION under     -- under, where (unter dem Bett / under the bed)
RELATION under-to  -- under, where to (unter das Bett / under the bed)
RELATION above     -- over, above, where (über dem Herd / above the stove)
RELATION above-to  -- over, where to (über den Herd / over the stove)
RELATION behind    -- behind, where (hinter dem Haus / behind the house)
RELATION behind-to -- where to (hinter das Haus)
RELATION front     -- in front of, where (vor dem Haus / in front of the house)
RELATION front-to  -- where to (vor das Haus)
RELATION beside    -- next to, where (neben dem Herd / next to the stove)
RELATION beside-to -- where to (neben den Herd)
RELATION between   -- between, where (zwischen den Häusern / between the houses)
RELATION through   -- through (durch den Park / through the park)
RELATION along     -- along (den Fluss entlang / along the river)
RELATION around    -- around (um den Tisch / around the table)
RELATION near      -- by, at someone's place (beim Bäcker, bei ihr / at the baker's, at her place)
# how, with what, with whom
RELATION with      -- with someone or something (mit ihrem Bruder, mit dem Messer / with her brother, with the knife)
RELATION without   -- without (ohne Zucker / without sugar)
RELATION by        -- by a vehicle, a means (mit dem Bus / by bus)
RELATION for       -- for someone or something (für ihn / for him)
RELATION about     -- about (über das Wetter / about the weather)
RELATION like      -- like, as (wie ein Kind / like a child)
RELATION of        -- a measure of a stuff, a part of a whole (eine Prise Salz / a pinch of salt; der Rand des Tellers)
RELATION smell-of  -- what something smells, tastes of (riecht nach Brot / smells of bread)
# when
RELATION at-hour   -- a clock time (um acht Uhr / at eight o'clock)
RELATION on-day    -- a day, a date (am Montag / on Monday)
RELATION in-period -- a month, a season, a part of the day (im Mai, im Sommer, am Abend / in May, in summer, in the evening)
RELATION since     -- since (seit zwei Jahren / for two years, since)
RELATION ago       -- ago (vor drei Tagen / three days ago)
RELATION in-time   -- after a span, from now (in zwei Wochen / in two weeks)
RELATION during    -- during (während des Konzerts / during the concert)
RELATION until     -- until (bis zum Abend / until the evening)
RELATION before    -- before (vor dem Essen / before the meal)
RELATION after     -- after (nach dem Essen / after the meal)
# why
RELATION because-of -- because of (wegen des Regens / because of the rain)
RELATION despite   -- despite (trotz der Kälte / despite the cold)
RELATION instead   -- instead of (statt des Busses / instead of the bus)
RELATION out-of    -- out of a feeling (aus Liebe, vor Freude / out of love, for joy)
