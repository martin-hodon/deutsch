# HOW ENGLISH SAYS A VERB'S ROLES (DESIGN.md), where the word does not take the defaults of en/rules/roles.txt -- as
# de/rules/frames.txt: `word = concept : item, item ...`. An item: `role marking` (direct, io, a preposition; `role`
# alone: its default; alternatives by the noun's kind as in roles.txt: goal on [surface] | into); `inf to|bare|ing|pp`
# (an infinitive's verb: goes to sleep, hears her sing, goes swimming, has it repaired); `that`, `that ing PREP`
# (looks forward to you coming); `role ={concept}` (the role holds exactly that noun, and the word says it: be
# hungry); `={concept}` (the plan's fixed= word, said by the word: be sorry); `role subj` (that role is the English
# subject, the plan's subject is not said: es tut mir leid -> I am sorry); `role {a|b}` (the role holds one of these
# nouns: crack = biť : patient {vajce}); `role [category]` (a noun of it, world/categories.txt: let = nechať : patient
# [person]; a person pronoun is a person). A concept's words are chosen by their lines: the first word whose line has
# the plan's roles; a word here that is not in the dictionary is an idiom of the concept (be sorry). Read by
# en/realize.js.
