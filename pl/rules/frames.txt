# HOW POLISH SAYS A VERB'S ROLES (DESIGN.md), where the word does not take the defaults of pl/rules/roles.txt -- the
# syntax of en/rules/frames.txt (`word = concept : item, item`): a role's marking is a CASE (B D C N Ms M) or
# `preposition+CASE` (o+Ms, na+B, do+D); `inf` an infinitive after the verb; `that` a że-clause, `that subj` a
# żeby-clause (the verb in the ł-form); `subj C` the plan's subject in the dative (podoba mi się); `={concept}` the plan's
# fixed word said by the word; `role ={concept}` the role holds exactly that noun and the word says it. A word that is an
# adjective of the dictionary is said as the predicate (jestem głodny); an adverb as an impersonal one (jest mi zimno).
