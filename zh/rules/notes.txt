# HOW CHINESE DOES EACH GRAMMAR STRUCTURE (engine/build.js: the grammar ladder shows the note of every structure a step
# brings; the names are world/syllabus.txt's). `structure = the rule, with a short example`.
present = Subject – verb – object; verbs never change: 狗睡觉。
predicate = An adjective is the predicate itself, usually with 很, no 是: 狗很老。 A noun takes 是: 他是老师。
object = The object follows the verb: 我看书。
adjective = Everything that describes a noun stands BEFORE it; a two-syllable adjective takes 的: 老狗, 漂亮的狗.
adjective-order = All before the noun: those with 的 first, then the bare ones next to the noun – one-syllable adjectives and words of origin or material (a kind of thing) – the most defining nearest: 漂亮的大黑狗, 新德国汽车.
one-gladly-very = 很, 非常, 真 before the adjective: 非常老. "Gladly" is 喜欢 + verb: 他喜欢跳舞。
possessive = Pronoun + 的 before the noun (的 is often left out with family): 我的狗, 我哥哥.
comparison = A 比 B + adjective (no 很): 狗比猫老。
past = No tenses: 了 after the verb for a completed action, or a time word: 狗看见了猫。
perfect = 了 after the verb (completed) or 过 (experienced): 她读了这本书。
future = 会 or 要 before the verb, or just a time word: 他明天会来。
modal = 想, 要, 能, 会, 必须 before the verb: 狗想睡觉。
negation = 不 before the verb or adjective; 没 for "have" and for past completed actions: 狗不睡觉。 他没来。
no-det = 没有 + noun: 我没有狗。
frequency = 常常, 总是, 有时 after the subject, before the verb: 他常常看书。
time-basic = Time stands before the verb, after the subject or first: 星期六, 五月, 八点. 狗今天在花园里睡觉。
time-phrases = 上个星期, 下个星期, 每天 – before the verb.
manner = Adjective + 地 before the verb: 安静地睡觉; or verb + 得 + adjective after it: 跑得快.
place-basic = 在 + place (+ 里/上) BEFORE the verb: 在花园里睡觉.
adverb-order = Before the verb: time – manner – place – verb: 狗今天安静地在花园里睡觉。
yes-no = 吗 at the end of the statement: 狗睡觉吗？
wh-basic = The question word stays where the answer would be: 狗在哪里睡觉？ 他什么时候来？
coord = Clauses joined by a comma, or 而且/但是: 她读书，他做饭。
that-because-if = 因为 ..., 所以 ...; 如果 ..., 就 ...: the reason or condition comes FIRST: 因为它累了，所以狗睡觉。
although-so-that = 虽然 ..., 但是 ...: the concession usually comes first.
relative = The relative clause stands before the noun with 的: 在花园里玩的狗.
