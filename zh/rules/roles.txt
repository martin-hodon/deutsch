# HOW CHINESE MARKS A ROLE (world/roles.txt) when a word does not say otherwise in zh/rules/frames.txt: `direct` the
# object after the verb; `pre X` a coverb phrase before the verb (给他 写信, 在家 吃饭, 跟朋友 一起去); `post X` after the verb
# (到北京); alternatives `|` by the noun's kind: [surface] [place] [container] [person] [vehicle] [thing]
patient     direct
second      direct
recipient   pre 给
as          post 当
bare        direct
theme       direct
topic       pre 关于
cause       direct
basis       pre 根据
purpose     pre 为了
companion   pre 和…一起
opponent    pre 跟
instrument  pre 用 | pre 坐 [vehicle]
share       direct
result      post 成
substitute  pre 用…代替
location    pre 在…里 | pre 在…上 [surface] | pre 在 [place] | pre 在…那里 [person]
goal        post 到 | post 到…那里 [person]
source      pre 从 | pre 从…那里 [person]
path        pre 穿过
