  ask   V.pres.*
  wrong V.pres.* ~V.pres.= V.impf.=

imperfect: imparfait
  steer tense=past freq=yes
  ask   V.impf.*
  wrong V.pres.= V.impf.* V.fut.=

future: futur simple
  when  tense=future
  ask   V.fut.*
  wrong V.cond.= V.fut.* ~V.fut.=

conditional: conditionnel
  when  mood=hypo
  ask   V.cond.*
  wrong V.fut.= V.impf.= V.cond.*

subjunctive: subjonctif
  when  link=although|so-that|before
  steer link=so-that link=before link=although
  ask   V.subj.*
  wrong V.pres.= V.subj.* V.impf.=

participle: participe passé
  ask   V.pp
  wrong ~V.pp V.inf V.impf.3sg

  ask   N.pl
  wrong ~N.pl N.sg

  ask   AN.*.*
